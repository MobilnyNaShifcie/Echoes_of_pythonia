The filenames above are normalized project names. They intentionally replace
the inconsistent names and duplicate extension found in the owner's working
asset directory.

The replacement `cursed_scarecrow` and `night_guard` files passed the production
gate as 32-bit PNG images with transparent corners. They replace the earlier
opaque RGB drafts, which were never added to the repository.

The neutral Seeker pair was approved by the project owner on 2026-08-24. Both
files are 1024×1536 32-bit PNG production derivatives with a genuine alpha
channel and transparent corners. The opaque source previews with a baked
checkerboard remain outside the project and are not production assets.

The female base Warrior and male Pierrot variants were approved by the project
owner on 2026-08-24. Both files are 1024×1536 RGBA production derivatives with
transparent corners, zeroed hidden RGB and clean chroma-extracted edges. The
male Heavy Knight remains a separate path-specific presentation asset.

The female and male Mage and Hunter variants were approved by the project owner
on 2026-08-24. All four files are 1024×1536 RGBA production derivatives with
transparent corners and normalized safe margins. They complete the base-class
combat-art matrix without assigning artwork to future class specializations.

The item icons form the Stage 9C golden slice approved by the project owner on
2026-08-23. They are normalized production derivatives of the approved source
artwork. Frames, rarity colors, quantities, labels, hover information and other
presentation metadata are generated by the UI and are not baked into the PNGs.
